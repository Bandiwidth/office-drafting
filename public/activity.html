PROCESSING

Background jobs

OCR, Excel imports/calculations and document downloads appear here. Retrying restarts the operation from saved local input and checks that its source is still current. Saved outputs and job history expire after 7 days or 50 finished jobs. Originals and saved documents are separate.

RECOVERY

Automatic local backups

Snapshots include saved letters, references, templates and workbooks. Credential files are excluded. Backups are checked before older verified copies are removed.

The schedule runs while the application service is running. If it was closed when a backup was due, the next launch catches up. Copies on the same laptop do not protect against loss of the laptop; download an extra copy when needed.

Import checks a downloaded .sqlite snapshot and adds it to this list; choose Restore as new copies to recover its documents. Restore adds new copies and does not replace existing work. It does not replay saved jobs or change your current provider settings. Recheck restored facts and financial mappings. Drafting Settings also supports the existing JSON backups.
