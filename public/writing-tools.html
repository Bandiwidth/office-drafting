Review both languages together
Enter English on the left, then click Translate to Telugu. The translation appears on the right using your selected AI provider. Typing alone does not send text.
Applying Telugu replaces the body wording and its formatting. The selected template retains control of the document layout.


Meeting minutes and actions
Only labelled notes are parsed automatically. Review the lists; nothing is inferred about decisions or responsibility. Creating minutes makes a new draft and keeps the source meeting.


Letter and annexures in one package
Choose references to include. Every attachment named in the letter must be selected.
Includes the Word letter, selected original attachments and a manifest with file checksums. It does not send correspondence or merge arbitrary layouts.


Prepare individual letters from Excel rows
Choose a workbook on the Excel → letter tab first. These are template-based drafts without AI. Review every preview; no letter is issued automatically.
Up to 50 rows. Existing office profile, document type and template are used. Facts are unconfirmed and approval evidence is cleared in each draft.


Create a searchable scanned PDF
Local Tesseract OCR and Skia PDF generation; no cloud. Up to 10 PDF pages or one image. Originals remain unchanged. OCR errors may remain, especially in names and Telugu. This is not redaction.
